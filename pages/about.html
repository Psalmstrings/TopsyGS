Who We Are

TGS (Eagles Height Consulting Ltd) is a premier professional surveying and geoinformatics firm providing high-precision cadastral, engineering, and hydrographic services across Nigeria. We combine technical excellence with cutting-edge technology to empower infrastructure, real estate, and energy sectors.

Our team of registered surveyors, GIS specialists, and geomatics engineers brings decades of combined experience, ensuring every project meets rigorous legal and quality standards. From land subdivision to complex setting-out for gas facilities, we deliver data you can build on.
